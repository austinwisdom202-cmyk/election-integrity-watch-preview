Independent evidence. Public accountability.

Election Integrity Watch is a non-partisan civic-technology initiative in development. This website is a fictional interface preview, not a public investigation or an official election platform.

The problem we aim to address

Election information can come from different sources and formats. People need a transparent way to compare recorded figures, preserve supporting documents, report discrepancies and distinguish verified findings from allegations.

What the operational system is being designed to do

Source-aware data intake

Accept records held with permission, with traceable origins. Integration with INEC is optional, not a dependency.

Independent comparisons

Flag arithmetical differences and changes between versions of documents for human assessment.

Evidence integrity

Preserve files privately with checksums, access restrictions and recorded review actions.

Responsible publication

Publish only reviewed, properly contextualised findings; include corrections and a right-of-response process.

What is ready, and what is not

Ready for demonstration: public pages, synthetic comparisons and a functional local prototype with protected reviewer workflows.

Not yet ready for public evidence collection: production security audit, legal and privacy sign-off, operational policies, reliable external sources and permanent protected backend hosting.

Areas where partners could help

Legal governance and data access; independent observer partnerships; cybersecurity and privacy assurance; sustainable hosting and operations funding; independent editorial oversight.

Independence: no affiliation, approval, endorsement or automatic live connection from INEC, the World Bank or any other organisation is implied.
