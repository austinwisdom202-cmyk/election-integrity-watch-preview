DATA PROTECTION

Privacy is part of the evidence standard.

This is an initial transparency notice. The eventual operating organisation must complete and approve the details before a public launch.

Pre-launch notice

Replace the placeholder controller identity, address and contact details. A qualified Nigerian data-protection adviser must approve the lawful basis, retention periods and any registration obligations before accepting real submissions.

Information collected

A report collects its category, state, optional location, factual description and optional source link. No contact details are requested. Administrators may privately add source documents for review; those documents can inadvertently contain personal information.

Purpose and access

Collected information is used to investigate electoral-record concerns, evaluate accuracy and publish only appropriately verified, redacted findings. Reports are private by default. Only authorised reviewers can access unpublished cases and private files.

Retention and rights

Retention and deletion schedules must be approved by the operating organisation. Requests for corrections, access, objections or erasure should be sent to privacy@example.org. Some records may require retention for defensible research or applicable legal obligations.

Security and disclosures

Access is role-limited, and files are not publicly served. A cryptographic checksum records the contents of evidence at upload. Public output contains approved findings only, not private source files. No biometric face recognition is used.

Independent operation

We are not affiliated with INEC. Any personal-data processing must comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and relevant 2025 implementation guidance.
