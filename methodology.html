PUBLIC METHODOLOGY

Trust is a process, not a claim.

Our methodology deliberately separates automated arithmetic screening from human-reviewed conclusions.

What the platform examines

Reviewers can compare independently obtained polling-unit result records against records attributed to official publications. The platform screens arithmetic and highlights conflicting reported totals. It does not have access to private INEC databases, BVAS devices, or ballots.

Important distinction

Inconsistent totals, questionable images and duplicate-looking records may justify investigation, but none independently proves unlawful voting, fabrication, or candidate intent. Secret ballots cannot be linked to identified voters through this application.

How evidence is handled

  1. Submissions enter a private queue and never publish automatically.
  2. Reviewers check source authenticity, timing, context, arithmetic and supporting documents.
  3. Files are stored privately with SHA-256 checksums; the file hash permits later integrity comparisons.
  4. Verification and publication are separate actions. Only administrators can release records.
  5. Public output includes corrections and contextual notes where necessary.

How calculations work

We flag: accredited voters exceeding registered voters; valid plus rejected ballots exceeding accreditation; and candidate vote totals that do not sum to reported valid votes. We also compare published and observer totals for matching polling-unit identifiers. Each is an investigation lead, never an automatic accusation.

Neutrality and rights

We scrutinise all parties and candidates by the same rules. We do not produce individual voter profiles, publicly publish voter-register photographs, automatically identify synthetic faces or secretly connect to electoral systems. We aim to redact personal information and provide an appropriate correction process.
